# Mixed strong-teacher d5/b256/500k game corpus v1

This versioned corpus contains games generated by the native Rust Pathfinder
teacher at depth 5, beam 256, and a 500,000-node budget on the teacher turns.
It contains 8,000 games against the same strong d5/b256/500k opponent and
2,000 games against a weaker d3/b64/12k opponent. The library uses a 20-ply
cap: the first four plies are seeded random openings for source diversity, and
every later teacher move uses the requested d5/b256/500k envelope. The
canonical game tables are produced by
`scripts/compact_game_corpus.py` from the validated
schema-v2 archive in
`research/20260901-strong-teacher-10k-games/workspace/mixed-10k/`.

`games/` is content-addressed by rules/configuration/action sequence and
`observations/` retains teacher/opponent provenance, outcomes, and source
metadata. The mixed source audit reports 10,000 unique full action sequences;
the final archive has no duplicate games or seeds.

Stable counts, search profiles, source hashes, and the duplicate policy are
recorded in `provenance.json`.
The raw JSONL, replay audit, split files, checkpoints, and training reports
remain disposable research artifacts rather than durable corpus tables.
